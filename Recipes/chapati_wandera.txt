TITLE      - Kenyan Chapati
AUTHOR     - Wandera Kisimbiri
CATEGORY   - Side
TOTAL TIME - 60 minutes

INGREDIENTS

2 cups wheat flour
1 cup warm water
2 tablespoons cooking oil
1/2 teaspoon salt

METHOD

1. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl.
2. Gradually add warm water and knead until you have a soft dough.
3. Add some oil and continue kneading.
4. Cover the dough and let it rest for 20 minutes.
5. Divide the dough into portions and roll each portion into a thin circle.
6. Cook each chapati on a hot pan, adding a little oil as needed.
7. Cook both sides until golden brown.
8. Serve warm.
