TITLE
-----
Lentil curry

AUTHOR
------
Vajira

CATEGORY
--------
Main / Side

SERVES
------
2

TOTAL TIME
----------
30 minutes

INGREDIENTS
-----------

Half a cup red lentils
Half a can coconut milk (thin)
250 ml water
Half of a small onion, thinly sliced
1 or 2 cloves garlic, chopped (optional)
small piece fresh ginger, grated (optional)
2 green chilies, slit lengthwise (optional)
5 curry leaves
half tsp black mustard seeds
2-3 whole cloves
half (or less) tsp turmeric poweder
half tsp curry powder (preferably Sri Lankan)
half tsp chili powder (optional)
half tsp red chili flakes (optional but recommended)
a few pieces of cinnamon
1 tbsp coconut oil
Salt, to taste
Juice of 1/2 lime (optional)

METHOD
------

- Rinse the red lentils under cold water until the water runs mostly clear and drain well.
- Heat the coconut oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat.
- Add mustard seeds and cook until they begin to pop.
- Add cinnamon, cloves and curry leaves. Fry for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
- Add onion and cook for 5 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
- Add garlic, ginger, and green chilies and cook for 1 minute.
- Add turmeric, curry powder, chili powder and chili flakes. Stir continuously for about 30 seconds to bloom the spices.
- Add the lentils, coconut milk, and water. Stir well and bring to a gentle simmer.
- Reduce heat to low. Cover partially with a lid.
- Cook for 20–25 minutes, stirring every few minutes.
- If the curry becomes too thick before the lentils are tender, add a little more water.
- Once the lentils are soft, season with salt.
- Give a splash of lime.

Serve with steamed rice. Pairs well with crusty bread too.
This is called a curry but personally I prefer it with very little curry powder and it tastes great with no curry powder at all too.
Vary the amount of coconut milk or even substitute it with think coconut milk or cream if you want it creamier. I lean leaner because it allows the spices to pop.
