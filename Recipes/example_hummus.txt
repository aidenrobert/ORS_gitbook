TITLE
-----
The greatest, creamiest hummus

CATEGORY
--------
Side

SERVES
------
1

TOTAL TIME
----------
1 hour

INGREDIENTS
-----------

200g dried spaghetti
100g guanciale or pancetta, cut into small cubes
2 large eggs
50g Pecorino Romano, finely grated (plus extra to serve)
1 clove of garlic (optional)
Freshly ground black pepper
Salt for the pasta water

METHOD
------

  1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.
     Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions
     until al dente. Reserve a mug of pasta water before draining.

  2. While the pasta cooks, fry the guanciale in a wide pan
     over medium heat until the fat has rendered and the edges
     are slightly crispy, about 5–7 minutes. Add garlic if using
     and cook 1 minute more, then remove garlic and take the pan
     off the heat.

  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the eggs and most of the
     Pecorino. Season generously with black pepper.

  4. Drain the pasta and add it to the pan with the guanciale.
     Toss quickly to coat the pasta in the fat.

  5. Pour the egg mixture over the pasta. Toss constantly,
     adding splashes of pasta water a little at a time, until
     you have a glossy, creamy sauce that coats every strand.
     Work quickly — the heat from the pasta cooks the eggs
     gently without scrambling them.

  6. Serve immediately with the remaining Pecorino and extra
     black pepper on top.


NOTES
-----
  - Never add cream. A real carbonara gets its creaminess
    from the emulsification of eggs, cheese, and starchy
    pasta water. Trust the process!
  - Guanciale (cured pork cheek) is traditional. Pancetta
    or streaky bacon work as substitutes but taste different.
  - The pasta water is crucial — the starch in it helps
    bind the sauce. Do not skip reserving it.
