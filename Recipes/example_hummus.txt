TITLE
-----
The creamiest hummus to exist

CATEGORY
--------
Side

SERVES
------
1

TOTAL TIME
----------
1 hour

INGREDIENTS
-----------

225 g dried chickpeas
2 tsp baking soda
Salt
1 small onion
1 small stalk of celery
2 cloves of garlic
2 bay leaves

350 mL tahini sauce (

	1 whole head garlic
	150 mL lemon juice
	1/2 tsp cumin
	300 g tahini
	Salt

	Blend all until smooth, adding cold water if needed.

)

METHOD
------

- Combine chickpeas, 1 tsp baking soda, and 2 tbsp kosher salt in a large bowl and cover with 1.4 L cold water.
- Stir to dissolve salt and baking soda.
- Let stand at room temperature overnight.
- Drain and rinse beans thoroughly.

- Place beans in a large pot.
- Add remaining baking soda, 1 tbsp salt, onion, celery, carrot, garlic, and bay leaves.
- Add 1.4 L water and bring to a boil over high heat.
- Reduce to a simmer, cover with lid slightly cracked, and cook until beans are completely tender, to the point of falling apart, about 2 hours.
- Check on beans occasionally and top up with more water if necessary.
They should be completely submerged at all times.

- Discard onion, celery, and bay leaves.
- Transfer chickpeas, carrot, and garlic to a food processor or high-powered blender with 250 mL cooking liquid.
- Cover blender, taking out the central insert on the blender lid.

- Place a kitchen towel over the hole in the center of the lid to allow steam to escape.
- Turn the blender to the lowest possible speed and slowly increase speed to high.
- If the mixture becomes too thick to blend, add cooking liquid a little at a time, until a very smooth, thick, and spreadable purée forms.
- Always start the blender on low speed before increasing to high.
- Continue blending until completely smooth.
- Transfer 250 mL cooking liquid to an airtight container and refrigerate.

- Transfer hot chickpea mixture to a large bowl.
- Whisk in tahini sauce and in salt to taste.
- Transfer to a sealed container and allow to cool to room temperature.
It should thicken up until it can hold its shape when spooned onto a plate.
- If puree is too thick, add reserved cooking liquid, 1 tbsp at a time, until hummus is desired consistency.

Serve with za'atar, paprika, warmed whole chickpeas, and/or chopped parsley.
Allow to come to room temperature before serving.
Leftover hummus can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.
